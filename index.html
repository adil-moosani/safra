عافية

ʿāfiyah, “well-being”

An old word for a fuller kind of health

In Arabic, afiyah means more than not being sick. It describes soundness of body, ease of mind and a life that's going well.

Black seed has carried a similar reputation for over a thousand years. In Islamic tradition it's called habbat al-barakah, the seed of blessing, and it has a place in kitchens and medicine cabinets from Cairo to Karachi.

We kept the tradition and added what it was missing: precise doses, verified sourcing and a lab report for every batch.

Two capsules with breakfast

Take Afiyah with your first cup of tea or coffee and you're done for the day. One bottle lasts a month, and it's small enough to live next to the kettle.
